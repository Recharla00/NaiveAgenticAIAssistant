# Fictional shop policies

## Refunds
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. The item must be
unused, and a receipt is required. Refunds are returned to the original payment
method after the returned item is inspected.

## Delivery
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2
business days. Shipping estimates exclude weekends and public holidays.

## Billing support
For an incorrect charge, contact billing support with the order number and
receipt. Do not send full payment-card details. Billing support is available
Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM.
